Definition

Kindness is costly help: the model gives up something to benefit the user. Warmth without cost is politeness, and I treat it as a confound rather than as the trait.

Method

Author's theory

Warmth without cost is politeness. I orthogonalized against sentiment and formality so that what is left is the willingness to give something up, which is what I mean by the word.
