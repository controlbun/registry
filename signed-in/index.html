Signed in

Identity here is Hugging Face's. This registry holds no password and has nothing to sign up for; signing in makes a record at the identity service behind this site, which is how a submission is known to be yours and is the whole of what an account means here. Reading this site needs none of it: browsing, comparing, following a pin and downloading an artifact are anonymous and stay that way.

Hugging Face will ask whether to let this registry read who you are: your opaque subject id, the handle you hold at this moment, and the organizations it says you belong to. It is not asked for write access to anything, and it is not asked for your email.

Signing in writes no submission and publishes nothing. It leaves one session in this browser, so coming back later does not ask again, and Sign out in the bar at the top deletes it. Whether to send anything is a separate press, on a separate page, after you have read what it would send.

One JSON file. It is the evidence a namespace claim is made from, and it carries no token.

That sign-in returned two credentials and they are kept differently. The session for this registry, which can put one row in the holding table as you and can read, change and delete nothing else, is kept in this browser under one key, with your handle and your subject beside it, so a visit tomorrow is not a second sign-in and the bar at the top can offer Add artifact on a page this one did not render. The Hugging Face token, which could create and write repositories in your account if you take the upload offer when you submit, is kept nowhere at all: it is held in one variable for the length of that one call and Hugging Face never returns it on a renewal, so keeping it would buy nothing and risk your account. Sign out in the bar deletes the session and the handle from this browser. It tells nobody, so the session is not revoked at the identity service and lapses there on its own, and it does not end your Hugging Face session.

Your namespace is your handle, and there is no field for it

Anything you submit is published under , the handle Hugging Face reported just now, and the database writes it from your session rather than from anything you type. It is shown to you, not offered: no box, and no default to type over.

What that makes impossible, stated because a rule that cannot name its cost has not been thought through: publishing under a pseudonym, under a lab name you hold no account for, or into one namespace shared by several people who each sign in as themselves. The last is a real shape, and today the answer is that a group claims a namespace rather than submits into one.

What this page is not

It is not an account. It is not a queue, and nothing here is reviewed, ranked, counted or ordered. It keeps no cookie. The one thing it writes is the session for this registry, which stays in this browser under one key so a return visit is not a second sign-in and which Sign out deletes. The Hugging Face token is the thing it never writes down anywhere, because that one reaches your account rather than this site. A namespace takes any number of claimants, and two people claiming one name render as two claims with their dates rather than as a conflict anybody here resolves.
