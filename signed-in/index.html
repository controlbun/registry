What this page is not
It is not an account. It is not a queue, and nothing here is reviewed, ranked, counted or ordered. It keeps no cookie and writes down no token. It writes two things and they are different in kind: the submission you send, which nobody else can read, and your handle in this browser, which is what the bar at the top reads and what Sign out deletes. A namespace takes any number of claimants, and two people claiming one name render as two claims with their dates rather than as a conflict anybody here resolves.
